Document requirements vary by programme and applicant. Check the current official admission notice for the required documents before applying.

### FAQ003: How do I check the status of my application?

Use the application-status method described in the official admission instructions, or contact the admissions office through a verified college channel.

### FAQ004: Can I change my programme choice after applying?
